The trend shows the general direction of a cryptocurrency over time – a negative trend indicates a downward movement while a positive trend indicates an upward movement. 

The moving average helps identify price trends by smoothing out minor fluctuations.

The correlation is a measure of the relationship between two cryptocurrencies.
