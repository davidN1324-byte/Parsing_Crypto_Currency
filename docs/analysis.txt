The correlation matrix towards the end offers insights into the relationships between pairs of currencies. A positive correlation suggests that the two currencies move in tandem, i.e., when one increases, the other tends to as well. A negative correlation suggests the two currencies move in opposite directions. The higher the absolute value of the correlation, the stronger the relationship.
